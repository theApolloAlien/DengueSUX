# DengueSUX brand kit

The mark combines a D silhouette, a neighbourhood roof and a focus point. It derives from the middle generated concept, simplified and rebuilt as SVG for small sizes. The dot is a fixed part of the mark, not a live-data indicator.

Use logo-mark.svg on ivory or white, logo-mark-dark.svg on forest. SVG wordmarks use font outlines so they render consistently without installed fonts. Use the wordmark variants at 24 px tall or above; leave at least one quarter of the mark height clear around it. Do not stretch or recolour priority status indicators as branding.

Typeface: locally vendored DM Sans. Main palette: forest #193e33, ivory #f7f8f4, sage #a9c799. Ochre belongs to minor illustration details and medium priority; terracotta belongs to high priority.

Illustrations are editorial depictions, not source data or actual mapped locations. They contain no figures or textual claims. Keep them outside the geographic map, chart and evidence layers. night-courtyard is a low-contrast backdrop; the data labels remain HTML.

The social card is 1200×630. neighbourhood-hero.webp and cover.png can be placed into a deck without modifying source artwork. hero.webp is a real screenshot with a dated source; update it when the app changes.

Generation: user-authorized codex-easy.ai fallback, gpt-image-2, high quality. Prompts, raw outputs and hashes: output/imagegen/denguesux/.
