Rules of the cascade that the layers would otherwise change are translated rather than lost: 59 of 98 `!important` declarations became plain declarations in the `overrides` layer (39 kept for `[hidden]`, print and reduced motion), 15 pure-element rules sit at the end of `base` so classes still outrank them, and 144 declarations that used to win on specificity over a later file were hoisted into `overrides` in source order. 137 `html body` prefixes removed, 14 duplicate rules dropped, breakpoints unified to 760/850/1000/1100/1250/1550, 411 text sizes lifted to the 12 px floor (sizes under 4 px, the icon-only trick, untouched).
