# DengueSUX experience direction

Extend the existing forest / ivory identity and DM Sans / Phosphor system. Keep geography, dated source facts, and the distinction between current area priorities and the 2022 national replay intact.

- Visible labels on view navigation, a short task-oriented introduction, and readable controls take precedence over extra decoration.
- Motion explains entrance, selection, a pending load, or an opened panel. No minimum artificial loading delay. Reduced motion disables decorative animation.
- The source date is interactive and opens methodology. Never label saved snapshots “live.”
- Map, forecast, and inspection data remain the existing source-backed pipeline artifacts. No generated imagery is used to represent geography or evidence.
- Inspection checklist progress reuses the existing dated session storage keys. Completion only describes checklist actions, never that an area is dengue-free.
- Responsive chat launcher is compact on phones and retains an accessible name. Larger controls and inputs support touch and keyboard use.
- Experience overrides are isolated in app/web/experience.css, loaded after the existing feature styles and included by the portable exporter.

No external asset generation or credentials were needed for this design pass.
